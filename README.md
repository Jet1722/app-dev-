# My Favorite Series: Sons of Anarchy

**Sons of Anarchy** is a gritty and emotional crime drama about a motorcycle club navigating loyalty, brotherhood, and survival.

## Why I Love It

- Intense action and raw emotion
- Deep character development
- Moral complexity and tough choices

## Main Characters

1. **Jax Teller** – The conflicted leader trying to steer SAMCRO to a better path.
2. **Clay Morrow** – The ruthless former president of the club.
3. **Gemma Teller** – The fierce and manipulative matriarch.

## Favorite Quote

> *"The only way to not be afraid is to have nothing to lose."* – Jax Teller
